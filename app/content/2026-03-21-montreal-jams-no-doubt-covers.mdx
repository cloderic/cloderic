Playing
[Dance Macabre at the Cabaret des Horreurs](./2025-11-28-eurythmics-the-white-stripes-7n7s)
last fall did something I more or less expected: it gave me the itch to get back
on stage, and this time not solo — as part of a band.

So early in 2026 I joined [Montréal Jams](https://montrealjams.com/) for their
winter session, and played for 10 weeks of rehearsal with strangers. Late March,
the result was a full set of **No Doubt** covers, an aptly named band:
**Reasonable Doubt**, a first show at the
[Piranha Bar](https://www.piranhabar.ca). A few weeks later we played our set
again at the [Notre Dame de Grace Porchfest](https://www.porchfestndg.com/).
