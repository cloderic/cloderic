After a run of covers, here's something different: an original tune.

Slow rock, spacy guitars, half asleep.

My son heard it and said it reminded him of a unicorn near a waterfall. She's
still in there, somewhere.

Everything you hear was written, performed, recorded, and mixed by me at home in
Montréal. It's still a demo, so expect it to evolve.

If you'd like to hear what comes next, subscribe on
[SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/cloderic)!
