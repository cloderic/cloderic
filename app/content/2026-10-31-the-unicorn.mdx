After a run of covers, here's something different: an original tune. And not
just any original: one I actually _finished_.

Like a lot of people who play, I have dozens of half-finished songs lying
around: riffs, chord loops, and fragments that sounded great one evening and
then sat untouched on a hard drive. This one could easily have joined them.

Then my son heard it and said it reminded him of a unicorn near a waterfall.
That image stuck with me. It gave the song a direction and was enough to push me
to finish it. She's still in there, somewhere.

Slow rock, spacy guitars, half asleep.

Everything you hear was written, performed, recorded, and mixed by me at home in
Montréal. It's still a demo, so expect it to evolve.

If you'd like to hear what comes next, subscribe on
[SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/cloderic)!
